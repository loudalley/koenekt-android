Koenekt

Delete your Koenekt account

For the Koenekt Android app and Koenekt People sign-in accounts.

How to ask

  1. Email william@koenekt.co.za from the email address linked to your account, or ask your organisation's Koenekt administrator.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my Koenekt account" and include your username and the organisation you use Koenekt with.
  3. We confirm the request with you or your organisation, then delete the login account within 30 days and confirm by email.

An organisation administrator can also disable a user immediately in People → Users; a disabled account cannot sign in, and its remembered sign-in on any phone stops working. Deleting the login record itself is done by Koenekt on request, as above. To remove the saved sign-in from your phone at any time, choose Sign out in the app, or uninstall it.

What is deleted

  • Your login account: username, password hash, display name, permissions and company access.
  • Your remembered sign-in, which stops working as soon as the account is removed.

What may be kept

Deleting a login account does not delete employment records that your organisation must keep, such as attendance, hours and payroll records. Those belong to your organisation and are kept for at least 1 year, or longer where employment or tax law requires it. The audit trail of changes made with the account is kept for 6 months for security. Ask your organisation if you want those records reviewed.
