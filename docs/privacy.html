Privacy policy

Koenekt Android app and the Koenekt People, licence and Reporter update services it connects to.
Effective: 20 October 2026

Who we are

Koenekt is operated by Koenekt (Pty) Ltd, South Africa ("Koenekt", "we"). Contact for privacy questions and requests: william@koenekt.co.za. Information officer: William, at the same address.

Koenekt provides attendance, staff, payroll and licensing services to organisations. When an organisation uses Koenekt to manage its staff, that organisation decides what staff information is recorded and is responsible for it; Koenekt processes it on the organisation's behalf. For your own login account, and for licence and customer records, Koenekt is responsible.

What stays on your phone

  • To keep you signed in, the app saves only the session token issued by the Koenekt services. It is encrypted with an Android Keystore key that requires your fingerprint (or other strong biometric) or your phone's PIN, pattern or password before it can be used. It is stored in app storage that is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.
  • The app never saves your password, authenticator codes or phone PIN. Fingerprint checks are handled by Android; the app never receives fingerprint data.
  • Reports you export are saved only to the location you choose. Temporary copies are deleted after saving.
  • Signing out deletes the saved session, its key and the app's cookies and web storage.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising or analytics SDKs, and no selling of personal information.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera or microphone. The app requests only Internet access and Android's biometric prompt.
  • Staff fingerprint or face enrolment for time clocks is done on the clocks, not through your phone.

Sharing

Information is shared with the organisation you work for or manage, within the permissions of each account, and with service providers who host the Koenekt services for us (Absolute Hosting, South Africa). We disclose information when the law requires it. We do not sell it.

Security

All connections from the app use HTTPS. Access is limited by account permissions and company access set by your organisation. Management accounts require an authenticator code.

How long information is kept

  • Login accounts: until you delete the account in the app (Account → Delete my account), your organisation deletes it, or you ask us to; emailed requests are completed within 30 days. After deletion only the username is kept, so the activity log stays accurate and the name can't be reused.
  • Attendance and payroll records: at least 1 year, and longer where your organisation must keep them under employment or tax law.
  • Audit and security logs (sign-ins, changes, IP addresses): 6 months.
  • The saved sign-in on your phone: until you sign out or uninstall the app, and it stops working when the server session expires (at most 30 days).

Your rights and requests

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or object to its processing. If your information is held by an organisation that uses Koenekt (for example your employer), contact that organisation first; we will help it respond. For your Koenekt login account, see Delete your account or email william@koenekt.co.za.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

Children

The app is for organisations and their staff and administrators. It is not directed at children.

Changes

We will update this page when the app or services change how information is handled, and change the effective date above.
